Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Common questions regarding consultation booking, document requirements, and consultation methods.

Q. How can I book a legal consultation?

You can book a consultation directly by calling +91 9687019503, sending a message on WhatsApp, or filling out the consultation request form on this website. Our chamber will confirm a suitable time slot for you.

Q. Can I discuss my case before visiting the office?

Yes, preliminary telephonic or WhatsApp consultations can be provided based on advocate availability. If the matter involves in-depth documents, an in-person chamber consultation is recommended.

Q. What documents should I bring for consultation?

Please bring all relevant documents related to your matter—such as agreements, legal notices received/sent, court summons, police complaints, land records, or ID proofs. Having documents organized in chronological order helps in prompt assessment.

Q. How can I contact the advocate directly?

You can call or WhatsApp directly on +91 9687019503 during working hours. For urgent court matters or emergency bail consultations, you can reach out via WhatsApp immediately.

Q. Is my consultation confidential?

Yes, 100%. All discussions, shared documents, and case inquiries are strictly protected under the statutory advocate-client privilege and confidentiality norms.
